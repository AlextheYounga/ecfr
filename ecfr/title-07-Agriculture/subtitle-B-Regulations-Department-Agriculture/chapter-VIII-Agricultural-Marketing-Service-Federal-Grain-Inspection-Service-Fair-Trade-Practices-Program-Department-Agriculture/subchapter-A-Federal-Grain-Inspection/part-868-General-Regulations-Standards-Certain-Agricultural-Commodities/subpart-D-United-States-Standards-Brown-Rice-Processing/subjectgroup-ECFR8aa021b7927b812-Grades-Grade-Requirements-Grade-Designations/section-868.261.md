##### § 868.261 Grade and grade requirements for the classes of brown rice for processing. (See also § 868.263.)

| Grade      | Maximum limits of— |
| ---------- | ------------------ |
| U.S. No. 1 | —                  |
| U.S. No. 2 | 2.0                |
| U.S. No. 3 | 2.0                |
| U.S. No. 4 | 2.0                |
| U.S. No. 5 | 2.0                |

| U.S. Sample grade                                                                                                                                                                | U.S. Sample grade shall be brown rice for processing which (a) does not meet the requirements for any of the grades from U.S. No. 1 to U.S. No. 5, inclusive; (b) contains more than 14.5 percent of moisture; (c) is musty, or sour, or heating; (d) has any commercially objectionable foreign odor; (e) contains more than 0.2 percent of related material or more than 0.1 percent of unrelated material; (f) contains two or more live weevils or other live insects; or (g) is otherwise of distinctly low quality. |
