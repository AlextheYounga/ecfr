##### § 102-118.245 How does my agency get an assigned set of GBL or GTR numbers?

If your agency does not use prenumbered GBL and GTR forms, you may get an assigned set of numbers from:

General Services Administration Federal Acquisition Service Inventory Management Branch (QSDACDB-WS) 819 Taylor Street, Room 6A00 Fort Worth, TX 76102\[65 FR 24569, Apr. 26, 2000, as amended at 74 FR 30476, June 26, 2009\]
