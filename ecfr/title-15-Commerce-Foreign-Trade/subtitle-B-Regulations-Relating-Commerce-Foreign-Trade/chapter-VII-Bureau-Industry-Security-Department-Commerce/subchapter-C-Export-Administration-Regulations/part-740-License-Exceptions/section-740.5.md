##### § 740.5 License Exception Syria Peace and Prosperity (SPP)

(a) *Scope.* License Exception SPP authorizes the export or reexport of all items subject to the EAR designated EAR99 to Syria.

(b) *Restrictions.* This license exception does not authorize exports or reexports, which otherwise require a license under any part 744 end-use or end-user control, including under § 744.8 for transactions involving persons designated on OFAC's Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List with certain identifiers specified under § 744.8(a)(1), unless authorized by OFAC or exempt.
