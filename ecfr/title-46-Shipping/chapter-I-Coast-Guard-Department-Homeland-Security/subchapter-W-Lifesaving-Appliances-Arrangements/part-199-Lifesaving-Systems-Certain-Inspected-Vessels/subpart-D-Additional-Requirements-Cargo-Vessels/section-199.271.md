##### § 199.271 Lifebuoys.

Each cargo vessel must carry the number of lifebuoys prescribed in table 199.271 of this section.

Table 199.271—Requirements for Lifebuoys on Cargo Vessels
| Length of vessel in meters (feet) | Minimum number of lifebuoys |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Under 100 (328)                   | 8                           |
| 100 (328) and under 150 (492)     | 10                          |
| 150 (492) and under 200 (656)     | 12                          |
| 200 (656) and over                | 14                          |
