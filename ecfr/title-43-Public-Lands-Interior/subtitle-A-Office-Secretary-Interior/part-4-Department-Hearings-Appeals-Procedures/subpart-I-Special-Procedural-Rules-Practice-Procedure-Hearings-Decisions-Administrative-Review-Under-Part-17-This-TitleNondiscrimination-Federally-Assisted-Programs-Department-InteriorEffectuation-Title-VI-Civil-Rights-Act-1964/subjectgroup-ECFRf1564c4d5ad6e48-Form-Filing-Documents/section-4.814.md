##### § 4.814 Certificate of service.

Link to an amendment published at 90 FR 2426, Jan. 10, 2025.This amendment was delayed until March 21, 2025, at 90 FR 9222, Feb. 10, 2025.This amendment was further delayed until May 5, 2025, at 90 FR 12461, Mar. 18, 2025.This amendment was further delayed to June 4, 2025, at 90 FR 18927, May 5, 2025.This amendment was further delayed until July 21, 2025, at 90 FR 24231, June 9, 2025.

The original of every document filed and required to be served upon parties shall be endorsed with a certificate of service signed by the party or amicus curiae making service or by his attorney or representative, stating that such service has been made, the date of service, and the manner of service.
