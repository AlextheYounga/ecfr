##### § 4.839 Exceptions.

Link to an amendment published at 90 FR 2427, Jan. 10, 2025.This amendment was delayed until March 21, 2025, at 90 FR 9222, Feb. 10, 2025.This amendment was further delayed until May 5, 2025, at 90 FR 12461, Mar. 18, 2025.This amendment was further delayed to June 4, 2025, at 90 FR 18927, May 5, 2025.This amendment was further delayed until July 21, 2025, at 90 FR 24231, June 9, 2025.

Exceptions to rulings of the administrative law judge are unnecessary. It is sufficient that a party, at the time the ruling of the administrative law judge is sought, makes known the action which he desires the administrative law judge to take, or his objection to an action taken, and his ground therefor.
