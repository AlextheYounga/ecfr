##### § 387.33T Financial responsibility, minimum levels.

Link to an amendment published at 83 FR 22877, May 17, 2018.

The minimum levels of financial responsibility referred to in § 387.31 are hereby prescribed as follows:

Schedule of Limits Public Liability

For-hire motor carriers of passengers operating in interstate or foreign commerce.

| Vehicle seating capacity                                           | Effective dates |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------- |
| (1) Any vehicle with a seating capacity of 16 passengers or more   | $2,500,000      |
| (2) Any vehicle with a seating capacity of 15 passengers or less 1 | 750,000         |

1 Except as provided in § 387.27(b).
