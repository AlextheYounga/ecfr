##### § 97.903 Measurements, abbreviations, and acronyms.

Measurements, abbreviations, and acronyms used in this subpart are defined as follows: 

BART—best available retrofit technology Btu—British thermal unit CO2—carbon dioxide CSAPR—Cross-State Air Pollution Rule H2O—water hr—hour lb—pound mmBtu—million Btu MWe—megawatt electrical NOX—nitrogen oxides O2—oxygen ppm—parts per million scfh—standard cubic feet per hour SIP—State implementation plan SO2—sulfur dioxide
