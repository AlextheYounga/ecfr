The Oregon plan was evaluated on the basis of the following classifications:

| Portland Interstate         | I         |
| Southwest Oregon Intrastate | II        |
| Northwest Oregon Intrastate | III       |
| Central Oregon Intrastate   | II        |
| Eastern Oregon Intrastate   | II        |
