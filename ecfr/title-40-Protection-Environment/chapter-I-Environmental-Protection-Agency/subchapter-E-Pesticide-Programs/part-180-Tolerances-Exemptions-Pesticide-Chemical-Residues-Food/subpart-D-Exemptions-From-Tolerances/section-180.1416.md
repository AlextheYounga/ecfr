##### § 180.1416 Pseudomonas oryzihabitans strain SYM23945; exemption from the requirement of a tolerance.

An exemption from the requirement of a tolerance is established for residues of *Pseudomonas oryzihabitans* strain SYM23945 in or on all food commodities when used in accordance with label directions and good agricultural practices.
