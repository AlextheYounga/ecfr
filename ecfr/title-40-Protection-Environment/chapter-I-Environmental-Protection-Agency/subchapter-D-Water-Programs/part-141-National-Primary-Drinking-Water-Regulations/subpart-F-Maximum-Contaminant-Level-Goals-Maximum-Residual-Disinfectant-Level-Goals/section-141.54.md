##### § 141.54 Maximum residual disinfectant level goals for disinfectants.

MRDLGs for disinfectants are as follows: 

| Disinfectant residual | MRDLG(mg/L)   |
| --------------------- | ------------- |
| Chlorine              | 4 (as Cl 2).  |
| Chloramines           | 4 (as Cl 2).  |
| Chlorine dioxide      | 0.8 (as ClO2) |
